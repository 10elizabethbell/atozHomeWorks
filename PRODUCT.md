# PRODUCT — AtoZ Home Works LLC

## Platform and stack
- Platform: web
- Stack: static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`), hosted on GitHub Pages. Only external resource: Cal Sans from Google Fonts.

## Users
- New Jersey homeowners who want work done in the house before the holidays: a room painted, drywall patched, a floor replaced, a kitchen or bathroom updated, or a list of handyman jobs. (Inferred from the ad's own hook, "Get your home holiday ready!")
- They find the business through Facebook (the ad was cross-posted to two NJ contractor groups on 2026-10-21) and open the link on a phone, inside Facebook's in-app browser.
- The owner of AtoZ Home Works, who receives this site and should recognise their own business in the first screen.

## Purpose
Turn a Facebook video ad into a place a homeowner can land, see what AtoZ does, and ask for a free quote in under a minute: by text (pre-written message) or by calling the number in the header.

## Positioning
A New Jersey home-improvement crew that covers the whole house, "from A to Z": painting, drywall, flooring, kitchens, bathrooms and handyman work. Seasonal hook from their own ad: book before the holiday rush.

## Capabilities
- Free quote request composed into a text message to 908-502-0457.
- Tap-to-call number in the header and in the closing band.
- Service rows that jump to the quote form with the job pre-selected.

## Constraints
- No real photos of the business's work exist yet. The ad video is stock/AI-style footage, not their jobs, so none of it is shown as their work.
- Whether the number accepts texts is unknown; calling must stay available everywhere texting is offered.
- No Facebook page URL, email, exact towns, hours, reviews, prices, insurance or licence number are known. None are shown or invented.

## Brand commitments
- Name as written on the ad's end card: "AtoZ Home Works LLC". (The Facebook posting name is "Atoz Home Works".)
- No logo exists; the name is set as a Cal Sans wordmark.
- Colours inferred from the ad's end card: dusk-blue sky and navy, warm amber window light and holiday bulbs.
- Their own words: "Get your home holiday ready!", "Book before the holiday rush!", "Call now for a free quote!", "Painting • Drywall • Flooring", "Kitchen & Bath Updates", "Serving New Jersey".

## Evidence on hand
- Facebook video ad (10 s, 720x1280), posted 2026-10-21 by "Atoz Home Works" to "CONTRACTORS NJ/PA Conncted" and "NEW JERSEY HOME IMPROVEMENT CONTRACTORS". End card reads: "AtoZ Home Works LLC · 908-502-0457 · Serving New Jersey · Book before the holiday rush!" Post text: "Call now for a free quote!"
- Muse brief, 2026-10-21.

## Principles
- Phone first: the first phone screen shows who, what, where and the free-quote action.
- Facts only. Inferences are marked inferred here and in HANDOFF.md.
- Speak as the crew ("we"), about how they treat the customer's home.
- Nothing presented as the business's own work until the owner supplies photos.
